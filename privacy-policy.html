We may use software integrations and automated workflows to help receive, route, organise or respond to WhatsApp messages. These tools support our communications and do not remove your rights in relation to your personal data.

We use WhatsApp for legitimate business communications, including responding to messages you send us, service communications and other communications where we have an appropriate lawful basis or consent where required.

Depending on the circumstances, we process personal data to respond to your request, take steps before entering into a contract, perform a contract, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate business interests, or where you have given consent.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and to meet legal, accounting, contractual or regulatory requirements.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
